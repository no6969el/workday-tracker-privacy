# Workday Tracker privacy policy

_Last updated: October 23, 2026_

Workday Tracker is a work log for field technicians: it reads your jobs from your calendar, keeps your
hours, records drives while you're clocked in, and helps you write your daily report. It has no account,
no server of its own, no ads and no analytics. What it stores stays on your phone unless you choose to
send it somewhere.

## What the app uses and why

**Calendar.** The app reads the events in the one calendar you pick as your work calendar, to list the
day's jobs (title, time, location and description). It doesn't change your calendar.

**Location.** Only if *Track drives* is on, and only from when you clock in until you clock out, the app
records your phone's location, including while the app is closed or not in use. It's used to:

- work out your drive miles and drive time for the day;
- learn where each job's house is (from where you were when you checked off its tasks), so Maps can take
  you to the right spot next time.

Location readings are kept on your phone and deleted after 45 days. The saved house spots stay until you
remove them in Settings → Addresses → Saved house spots. Location is never sent to Gemini or anyone else.
You can turn off *Track drives* in Settings at any time.

**Contacts.** The app reads your contacts only to tell whether a customer's phone number is already saved,
so it can offer *View contact* instead of *Add contact*. Adding a contact opens your phone's contacts app
with the customer's details filled in; you decide whether to save it.

**Files you pick.** Restoring a backup or importing a Google Maps Timeline export (to add past drives)
reads the file you choose, on the phone.

**Notifications.** While you're clocked in with *Track drives* on, Android shows a notification that the
app is recording.

## Gemini (optional)

If you enter your own Google Gemini API key in Settings, the app sends text to Google's Gemini service
when you ask it to write a report or when it sorts a job's tasks: the jobs' titles, addresses as written,
descriptions, tasks, your notes and the day's hours. These usually include customer names and may include
phone numbers. The requests go straight from your phone to Google under your key, and Google's terms for
the Gemini API apply to them.

Without a key, the app uses the phone's own built-in AI model when the phone has one (Gemini Nano on
supported Android phones, Apple Intelligence on supported iPhones). That runs entirely on the phone, so
nothing is sent anywhere. On phones without one, the app sorts tasks and writes the report itself, also
on the phone.

## What leaves your phone, and only when you choose

- **Reports** are sent with your email app, to whoever you address them to.
- **Backups** are saved in the app's folder on your phone. *Share* sends one through the app you pick.
- **Google Maps** gets the address (or saved spot) of a job when you tap it to navigate.
- **Phone and messages** open your dialer or messaging app with the customer's number.

## Sharing and selling

The developer receives none of your data. Nothing is sold or shared with advertisers or data brokers.

## Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored, except backup files you shared or copied elsewhere.
You can also remove saved house spots individually in Settings.

## Children

The app is a work tool and isn't meant for children.
